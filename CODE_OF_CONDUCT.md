# Code of conduct

Somnoscribe contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Treat clinical-safety and data-protection concerns with care.
- Stop sharing material if its provenance or sensitivity is questioned.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

Good-faith disagreement about technical, safety, licensing, or clinical-risk questions is welcome when expressed respectfully and supported with evidence.
