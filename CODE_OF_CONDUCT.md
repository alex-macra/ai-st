# Code of conduct

Participation must be respectful, constructive, and safe.

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, licensing, and clinical-safety boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or disrupt participation.
- Stop sharing material when its provenance or sensitivity is questioned.

This applies in project spaces and when representing Somnoscribe elsewhere. Report concerns privately to the maintainers. They may remove content, restrict participation, or take proportionate action; retaliation is prohibited.
